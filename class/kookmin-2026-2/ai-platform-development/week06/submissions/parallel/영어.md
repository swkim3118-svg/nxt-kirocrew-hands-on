# Department AI Hands-On Practice Meetup

## Summary

We are holding a hands-on practice meetup where you can use AI directly together with fellow students from the department. There are 12 spots available, and students who are completely new to AI practice are welcome to join. The English and Japanese guides are provided only to help you understand the meetup details — the guidance and all hands-on activities on the day of the meetup will be conducted entirely in Korean.
